    How to

    1. Lay the phone flat on the string, keel line or gunwale along the boat's centreline, top edge towards the stern. Tap Zero centreline.
    2. Lay the phone flat on the oar shaft, top edge towards the blade. Move the oar to the catch and tap Save catch, then the same at the finish.
    3. Negative = catch (blade towards the bow, handle towards the stern), positive = finish. 0° = square to the boat.
    4. The gyro drifts a few tenths of a degree per minute. Re-zero before each seat and keep the phone away from steel riggers and magnets.

    Camera bump calibration

    The iPhone's camera bump keeps it from lying flat. Put the phone on a stable surface and tap Measure A. Turn it 180° on the same spot and tap Measure B. The error is then removed from every tilt reading.

    Marks & gearing

    Marks are measured along the boat's centreline from the square-off position of the pin. Sweep: spread × tan(angle). Scull: half span × tan(angle). Handle reach = inboard × sin(catch angle).

    Blade pitch by plumb line

    Level the boat. Hold the oar square to the boat at rowing depth, with the back of the sleeve pressed against the swivel. Hang a plumb line over the blade 5–10 cm from the tip and measure the horizontal distance from the blade's lower edge to the line. Ideally check at catch and finish too.

    What to change
